# gjPDFs Updated on the App Store

[**gjPDFs**](/projects/gjPDFs) has been updated to **v2026.12** on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/gjpdfs/id6760037275).

This is an maintenance release with the following changes:
- Fixed table of contents changes sometimes not being saved.
- Fixed the sidebar's table of contents and thumbnails buttons getting cut off when the sidebar was narrow.
- Can now make the sidebar narrower to leave more room for documents.
