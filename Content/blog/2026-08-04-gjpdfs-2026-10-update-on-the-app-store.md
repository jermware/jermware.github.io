# gjPDFs Updated on the App Store

[**gjPDFs**](/projects/gjPDFs) has been updated to **v2026.10** on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/gjpdfs/id6760037275).

This is an major enhancement release with the following changes:
- Open multiple PDFs at once, each in its own window.
- Documents can now open as tabs, respecting your macOS "Prefer tabs when opening documents" setting.
- Close a single tab with Cmd+W, or the whole window with Cmd+Shift+W.
- New windows cascade into view and remember their size and position between launches.
